## Step 2: Choose your hackathon type

*Tick one. If you're not sure, pick the smaller one. You can move up next time.*

|  | **Type** | **Format** | **People** | **Budget** | **Lead time** | **Best for** |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | **Tier 1: Open Build** | Weekday evening, 2.5–3 hrs | 20–60 | \$0–300 | 2–3 weeks | Showcasing a tool and building a community habit. No theme, no judges, no pressure. |
| ☐ | **Tier 2: Themed Sprint** | Saturday, 5–6 hrs | 50–120 | \$300–1,500 | 5–6 weeks | One broad theme, small teams, 3-minute demos, 3 judges, small prizes. |
| ☐ | **Tier 3: Civic Build** | Full day, or 2–4 weeks | 100–250 (or smaller) | \$1,500–8,000+ | 10–12 weeks | Real problems raised by the community, with a named local organisation that wants the result. |

**What each type needs from you:**

- **Tier 1:** a room with wifi, pizza, and optionally a 10–15 minute tool talk.
- **Tier 2:** everything in Tier 1, plus a theme idea, 3 judges, and small prizes or credits.
- **Tier 3:** everything in Tier 2, plus a local civic, NGO or public-sector partner, a mentor bench, and datasets or APIs shared **one week before** the event.

## Step 5: Ground rules (please confirm)

- ☐ The event follows the AI Collective **Code of Conduct** and **Data Privacy Policy**.
- ☐ **Non-technical people are welcome.** Mixed teams are encouraged, and required at Tier 3.
- ☐ Participants are **not required** to use our product to take part. It can only be a condition for a specific prize, and only at Tier 2 or 3.
- ☐ We will **not** collect attendee data or leads as a condition of entry.
- ☐ Only work built **during the event** counts. Participants can bring ideas but not pre-written code.
- ☐ **Tier 3 only:** no commercial outcome is expected. We fund capability, not conclusions.
